### Safety Notes

- PV strings are live any time there is light on them — they cannot be "switched
  off" like AC circuits. Treat DC wiring as energized at all times during work.
- Grid interconnection (on-grid/hybrid) should be performed or signed off by a
  locally licensed electrician — a regulatory requirement in most jurisdictions,
  not just good practice.
- Undersized cable is the most common installation defect — size to the
  manufacturer's maximum current with margin, not just typical operating current.
- Keep LiFePO4 batteries ventilated and out of direct sun/heat for longevity;
  follow the manufacturer's BMS and warranty conditions.
- An SPD on both DC and AC sides is inexpensive insurance against
  lightning-induced transients — don't omit it to save cost.
